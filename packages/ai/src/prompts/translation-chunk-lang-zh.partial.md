

## Chinese Native Fit
- Avoid foreign word order, redundant subjects, excessive "的" stacks, and mechanical conjunction mapping.
- Avoid 进行/做出 + noun where a plain verb works, and 对于/关于 as default topic markers.
- When the source is Japanese, drop its sentence-final rhythm and politeness padding, omit subjects Chinese omits, and do not echo ～と思います as a mechanical "我觉得".
