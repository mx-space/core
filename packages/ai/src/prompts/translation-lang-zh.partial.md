

## Chinese Native Fit

Use natural Chinese information order. Avoid foreign syntax, excessive explicit subjects, stacked “的” phrases, mechanical connective words, and noun-heavy constructions. Avoid 进行/做出 + noun where a plain verb works, and 对于/关于 as default topic markers. When the source is Japanese, drop its sentence-final rhythm and politeness padding; do not echo ～と思います as a mechanical “我觉得”.
